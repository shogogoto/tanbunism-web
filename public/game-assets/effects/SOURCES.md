# Skill animation sources

These are third-party animation assets hosted locally, not generated by this project.
Licenses differ by pack; see the sections below. No external URLs are loaded at runtime.

## Additional ice, lightning and spell materials (2026-10-25)

- Willosuna, [Magic Spells VFX Lite](https://willosuna.itch.io/magic-spells-vfx-lite),
  `magic-spells-vfx-lite.zip`, manifest version 2.0.
- Imported six distinct core animations: `ice_shatter`, `zap`, `fire_impact`,
  `cast`, `heal`, `impact`. IDs are `willo_<original name>`.
  The nine elemental recolors and white variants are deliberately not imported.
- Original manifest frame durations are used, with a 650ms minimum for visibility.
  Pixel edges are preserved with nearest-neighbor scaling. The looping heal is
  played once per action, just like other combat effects.
- License: royalty-free for personal/commercial game use, **not CC0**.
  The complete bundled terms are preserved in `WILLOSUNA-LICENSE.txt`.
  These converted images are embedded game effects, not a standalone asset pack.
  Do not redistribute them as a library or asset collection.
- Matching core SE are imported from the same author's Magic Spells SFX Lite.
  This project did not generate, recolor or synthesize these assets.

- StarsteelGaming, [Spell Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/spell-effects-by-starsteelgaming),
  [Spell Effects.zip](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Spell%20Effects.zip), CC0.
  IDs: `oga_star_ice` (Icespear, 6 frames), `oga_star_thunder` (Thundersphere,
  8 frames), `oga_star_fire` (Fireball, 9 frames). Preserve all frames and
  64x32 aspect ratio, nearest-neighbor scaling into transparent 192px cells.
- These additions do not change existing IDs or saved animation selections.
- To reproduce only the new effects, extract Willosuna into `vfx/` and
  Starsteel into `starsteel/`, then run:
  `node scripts/import-skill-effects.mjs <download-directory> willo_ice_shatter,willo_zap,willo_fire_impact,willo_cast,willo_heal,willo_impact,oga_star_ice,oga_star_thunder,oga_star_fire`.
  The optional comma-separated IDs preserve all other manifest entries and files.

- Creator: Mochi Lab Studio.
- Source: https://mochilab-studio.itch.io/game-fx-starter-vol1
- Download: `game-fx-starter-vol1-asset-bundle.zip`, version 1.1.0, retrieved 2026-10-17.
- Local IDs: `mochi_status_curse`, `mochi_status_buff`, `mochi_status_shield`,

- Original names: `starter_status_curse`, `starter_status_buff`,
  `starter_status_shield`, `starter_status_heal`, `starter_status_speed`.
- License (from the bundled README and the source page):
  "Royalty-free for commercial use. Attribution appreciated but not required.
  No resale of source files."
- Only these five status animations are embedded in the game. This is not a
  redistribution of the original asset bundle or a standalone asset product.
  The original license continues to apply to the embedded derived images;
  do not treat them as CC0 or resell them as source assets.
- Processing: preserve all 24 original frames, downscale from 512px to 192px,
  append a transparent final frame, encode as lossless WebP; use frame 12 for
  the 96px poster. No recoloring or AI-generated variants.

## Processing

- Keep original ordering (numeric filenames; row-major 128px sheet cells).
- Pad each animation's frames to its largest canvas, fit into a transparent
  192×192 cell, and append horizontally into lossless WebP strips.
- Add one transparent final frame. Posters are 96×96 representative frames.
- `app/features/game/effectSpriteManifest.json` records frame counts, frame size,
  playback duration and authors.
- Rebuild with `node scripts/import-skill-effects.mjs <download-directory>`.
  Extract `Classic.zip` under `slash/`, `FX.zip` under `magic/`, and put the five
  Soluna sheets at the download directory root. ImageMagick is required only
  when importing assets, not at build time or runtime.
- Extract the Nature, Arcane, Earth Impact and Cosmic Time ZIPs under `nature/`,
  `arcane/`, `earth/` and `cosmic/` respectively. Nature files use a numeric
  prefix per animation (`0-` through `3-`); exclude its `Preview.png`.
  Arcane uses subdirectories `01`–`06`, Earth and Cosmic use `1`–`5`.
- The twenty additional effects keep their original frames (no recoloring).
  Their posters use a later representative frame (60% through the sequence)
  so expanding plants, magic circles and rocks are recognizable in the gallery.
- Extract the Mochi Lab ZIP under `mochi/`, retaining its top-level
  `game-fx-starter-vol1/frames/starter_status_*/` directories. Only those five
  status sequences are imported; other categories and original archives are
  not included in the repository.

The in-app Japanese preset names describe the effects; they are not original titles.
